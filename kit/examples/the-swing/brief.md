# Video #9 — "The Swing"

- **Made:** Nov 10, 2026 (Claude, for Kai's approval). Not posted.
- **Ask:** Kai shared a pendulum reel (@1keysolution, "Need a website? → Let's talk!") and said "make me something like this"; on Nov 10 he asked for "a really good video for my business". This is that pendulum idea with our own words, design and music.
- **Idea:** the Horizon Scales logo badge hangs on a coral string and swings like a pendulum. Each pass wipes one card into the next, with the string as the edge of the wipe.
- **Script (on-screen text only, no voice):**
  1. Hook: "You never hear about the jobs you lose."
  2. Leak 1 of 3: "They can't find you." → The fix: "We run your Google & Meta ads."
  3. Leak 2 of 3: "Your website doesn't get the call." → The fix: "We build one that does."
  4. Leak 3 of 3: "You miss calls on the job." → The fix: "Our AI receptionist picks up and books it."
  5. Payoff: "So you get found, called and booked."
  6. End card: logo, "You run the business. We help it scale.", "Websites · Ads · AI receptionists", horizonscales.com.
- **Look (new for this video):** cream paper background, Young Serif type in dark ink, coral underline that draws itself under each fix, small spaced labels ("LEAK 1 OF 3", "✓ THE FIX"). At the end the badge stops in the middle and its plum disc blooms into the standard plum end card, where it becomes the logo.
- **Timing:** 120 BPM. Each pass is 4 beats (2 s) with hang time at each side, so every card is fully readable for about 1.4 s; the hook holds for 2.25 s. 21 s total.
- **Music (original, synthesized):** swung boom-bap in F major. A clock tick on every beat, a whoosh as the logo passes, a wooden tock when it turns, minor chords on leaks and major on fixes, a bell on each fix, and a resolve to F when the logo blooms.
- **Frame safety rules (Kai, Nov 5):** words stay inside x 140–860, y 290–1540. The swing is centred at x 435 (left of the middle) and travels 325 px each side, so the logo's edge stops at about x 860 and never enters the right-side button strip. Nothing sits in the bottom strip. A small zoom punch lands with every card. Backgrounds are flat, so no text stroke is needed.
- **Honesty check:** no stats, results, testimonials or business names; no prices, guarantee or opening date; "found, called and booked" states the aim, not a promise; the receptionist is named as AI.
- **Checks:** render is deterministic; 1080×1920, 30 fps, 21.00 s; −14.1 LUFS, true peak −1.4 dBTP; no black frames; text-framing check clean. Claude cannot hear the audio; the mix was checked by measurement.
- **Files:** `index.html` (animation), `timeline.js` (script and timing), `score.mjs` (music). Assets: `badge.png` (round logo badge) and `wordmark.png` (cut from `scales.png`). Outputs: `the-swing.mp4`, `the-swing-cover.jpg`.
