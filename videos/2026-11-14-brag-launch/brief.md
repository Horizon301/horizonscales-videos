# Brag plan: Horizon Scales

**Input:** website, horizonscales.com (live site, read Nov 14, 2026) + HQ brand files.
**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 20.0 s. **Tone:** polished. **Focus:** the three services working together.

## Answers
- **What is it:** a one-person agency in Boise that builds your website, runs your Google/Meta ads, and sets up an AI receptionist that answers when you can't.
- **Who for:** local service business owners (trades, home services, shops) who lose jobs to missed calls and invisible ads.
- **What sets it apart:** all three pieces built and managed together; month-to-month; you own your accounts, website and data; first 30 days of live ads free.
- **Most impressive claim (from the site):** the AI receptionist picks up at 7:42 PM while the owner is on a job and books the estimate.
- **Visual hook:** a phone ringing at 7:42 PM while you're on a job, with the site's dusk-and-sunset look.
- **Real UI shown:** the site's own AI receptionist call example (Sarah M., paver patio, Saturday 11:30 booked), the three service cards with their real taglines, the plan builder price.
- **Share caption:** "You're on a job. Your phone rings. Who answers?"

## Angle
The hero sentence of the site, told as one evening: the call you'd miss, caught. Then pull back to show it's one piece of a system (ads bring them, the site turns them into calls, the AI catches the call). End on the tagline.

## Rules applied
- No "Opening spring 2027" and no "book now" / pitch (Kai's video rules, Nov 3). End card = logo, tagline, services, horizonscales.com.
- The AI call is the site's labeled example ("An example of the AI receptionist we set up for clients"); the receptionist is shown as AI. Names are the site's fictional examples. No stats, results or testimonials.

## Music
96 BPM (beat 0.625 s, bar 2.5 s), A minor, warm synth-pop. 8 bars = 20 s. Every cut on a beat.
