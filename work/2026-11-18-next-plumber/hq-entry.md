**New rule (written into `RUNBOOK.md` section 2b-3, "Plain and clear beats clever"):**
- No wrapper around the idea (no karaoke song, crayon puzzle, poster or game-show dressing). Show the real thing: the phone, the website, the search result, the person.
- Any single frame, with the sound off, must make sense to a trade owner in 3 seconds.
- One thing on screen at a time, at most 8 captions, each up at least 2.4 s, plain words.
- Big topics only (missed calls, not being found, a website that doesn't get the call), not small details.
- Games only when the game is the plain thing itself. A clear hook beats a high hook score.
